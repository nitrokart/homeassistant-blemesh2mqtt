Troubleshooting & Guides

Devices not appearing in Home Assistant

Verify that the MQTT pill in the top header is green (Online). Home Assistant automatically discovers devices through MQTT discovery under the homeassistant/light/ topic. Check the Entities page in HA Settings.

How to reset and re-pair a bulb

Turn the light off and on 3 to 5 times (approx. 1 second per toggle). The bulb will flash rapidly or pulse to indicate factory reset mode. Run scan in the Add Device tab immediately.

Raspberry Pi 4 Bluetooth controller advice

The onboard Raspberry Pi 4 Broadcom chip has known BlueZ packet transmission constraints. Ensure your add-on option io is configured to generic if needed.

Mesh relay best practices

Mains-powered bulbs and LED drivers make excellent mesh repeaters. Battery-powered sensors or remotes should always have relay disabled to conserve power.
